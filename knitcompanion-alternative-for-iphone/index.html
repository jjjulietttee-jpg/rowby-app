FAQ

What is the cheapest knitCompanion alternative?

Based on store listings, Stitch Keeper is a one-time $4.99 and StitchTally has paid options from $0.99 per week, $9.99 per year or $24.99 lifetime. Check the listings for current prices.

What does knitCompanion do better?

Per its listing it offers chart tools, Ravelry and Dropbox links and iCloud sync, with a one-time paid unlock rather than a subscription.

What does Rowby do differently?

It keeps your own PDF, photo or scan on screen with a ruler that follows your count, plus on-device voice counting and linked repeat counters.

Is Rowby cheaper than knitCompanion?

Not necessarily. Rowby Pro is $29.99 per year, $4.99 per month or $59.99 lifetime in the US, while knitCompanion's one-time unlock is listed around $29.99. Rowby is free to try with 30 rows.

Does Rowby support chart symbols?

No. Rowby follows your pattern as an image or PDF with a ruler and does not read charts.

Does Rowby sync with iCloud or Ravelry?

No. It has no cloud sync and no Ravelry integration, and you can export a project as a file.

Which should a crocheter choose?

For simple counting, a cheap counter. For following a PDF row by row with a ruler, Rowby. For deep chart and library tools, knitCompanion.

Can I try Rowby first?

Yes. Your first project and first 30 counted rows are free.
