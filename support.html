Common questions

  • How do I set the ruler? Open your pattern, tap “Set ruler”, tap the row you are on, then tap the next row below it.
  • The counter went up twice. Rowby ignores double taps closer than a tenth of a second. Use Undo, or History → “Undo to here”, to go back any number of steps.
  • Voice counting doesn't hear me. Allow the microphone and speech recognition in Settings → Rowby. Say “next”, “back” or “undo”.
  • Restore purchases. Settings → Rowby Pro → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.
  • Cancel a subscription. Settings on your iPhone → your name → Subscriptions → Rowby.
