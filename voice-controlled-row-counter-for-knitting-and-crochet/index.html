Voice-Controlled Row Counter for Knitting and Crochet

Rowby has hands-free counting for iPhone: say 'next' to add a row, 'back' to subtract one, or 'undo' to revert, and keep both hands on your hook or needles. Speech is recognised on your device and nothing is recorded. Voice counting is part of Rowby Pro.

Get Rowby on the App Store

Steps

  1. Open your project. Open the project in Rowby.
  2. Allow microphone and speech access. Rowby asks for permission the first time.
  3. Turn on hands-free. Switch on hands-free counting for the project.
  4. Say a command. Say 'next', 'back' or 'undo'.
  5. Check the count. Glance at the big count; the pattern ruler moves with it.

FAQ

Which words does Rowby listen for?

'Next' adds a row, 'back' goes back one and 'undo' reverts the last step. Rowby listens for a few synonyms such as 'plus' and 'minus' as well.

Is the speech sent to a server?

No. Rowby requires on-device speech recognition and does not record audio.

Does it work offline?

Yes, as long as on-device speech recognition is available for your language on your iPhone.

Is voice counting free?

It is part of Rowby Pro. You can try the counter free with your first project and 30 rows.

What does Pro cost?

US prices: $29.99 per year with a 7-day free trial, $4.99 per month, or $59.99 once.

Are there alternatives to voice counting?

Yes. Rowby has a large tap pad and a Lock Screen +1 button; the screen stays awake. Other counters usually rely on taps or volume buttons.

Does voice work in noisy rooms?

Recognition quality depends on the environment and your iPhone. Tapping always works as a fallback.

Is a voice counter worth it for beginners?

It helps most when your hands are busy, for example on tight amigurumi rounds. Beginners can start with taps.
