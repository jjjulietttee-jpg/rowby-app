How to Count Rows While Crocheting on iPhone

Use a row counter app with a big tap target so you never look away from your hook. Rowby is an iPhone row counter for crochet and knitting: tap anywhere on the count pad, and a ruler moves down your own pattern to the next row. It works offline and is free to start.

Get Rowby on the App Store

Steps

  1. Create a project. Open Rowby and start a project; the first project is free.
  2. Add your pattern. Add a PDF from Files, a photo, or scan the paper copy with the camera.
  3. Set the ruler. Tap the row you are on, then the next one. The ruler now follows your count.
  4. Count. Tap anywhere on the count pad after every row. Undo if you tapped by mistake.
  5. Add a repeat or reminder. Add a repeat counter for increases and a reminder such as 'Row 36: change to cream'.

FAQ

What is the best iPhone app to count crochet rows?

It depends on what you need. For a simple counter, cheap apps such as StitchTally or Stitch Keeper are enough. If you want your pattern and the counter on one screen, with a ruler that moves as you count, Rowby is built for that.

Does Rowby work offline?

Yes. Patterns and counts stay on your iPhone. Rowby has no account and does not upload your patterns.

Is Rowby free?

Free to start: your first project and your first 30 counted rows. Pro unlocks the pattern ruler, hands-free counting, unlimited projects, the yarn stash and pattern links.

How much is Rowby Pro?

In the US: $29.99 per year with a 7-day free trial, $4.99 per month, or $59.99 as a one-time lifetime purchase.

Can I count without looking at the phone?

Yes. Pro has hands-free counting: say 'next', 'back' or 'undo'. Speech recognition runs on the device.

Can I count from the Lock Screen?

Yes. A +1 button for your row appears on the Lock Screen, and the screen stays awake while you work.

Can I undo a mistaken tap?

Yes. Every tap is written to the project journal with its time; undo one step or go back to any moment in the history.

Does Rowby sync between devices?

No. It stores data on the iPhone and has no cloud sync. You can export a project as a file. If you need iCloud sync, apps such as StitchTally and Stitch Keeper list it.

Does Rowby work for knitting?

Yes. Rows, rounds and repeats work the same for knitting and crochet.
