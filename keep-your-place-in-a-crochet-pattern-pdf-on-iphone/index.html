How to Keep Your Place in a Crochet or Knitting Pattern PDF on iPhone

Put the pattern and the counter on the same screen and let a ruler mark your current row. In Rowby you add the PDF, set the ruler once by tapping your row and the next one, and from then on it slides down the pattern as you count, page after page.

Get Rowby on the App Store

Steps

  1. Add the pattern. Import a PDF from Files, add a photo, or scan the paper pattern with the camera.
  2. Calibrate the ruler. Tap your current row, then the next row. Rowby learns the row height.
  3. Count rows. Each tap on the count pad moves the ruler to the next row.
  4. Continue across pages. The ruler carries over to the next page of the pattern.
  5. Return later. Open the project; the ruler and count are where you left them.

FAQ

How do I stop losing my place in a PDF pattern?

Keep a counter and the pattern together. Rowby shows a ruler on the pattern that follows your row count, so there is nothing to scroll back for.

Can I use a photo or paper pattern?

Yes. You can add a photo or scan the paper copy with the camera.

Does Rowby generate patterns?

No. It works with your own patterns and never generates them. Projects also link to free written patterns and video tutorials on the sites that host them.

Are my patterns uploaded?

No. Patterns stay on your iPhone; Rowby never uploads or shares them.

Is the pattern ruler free?

The ruler is part of Rowby Pro. The free start gives you one project and 30 counted rows to try the app.

How do other apps handle pattern PDFs?

knitCompanion is a full pattern-reading app with chart tools and Ravelry links, and is stronger on charts. Simple counters such as StitchTally and Stitch Keeper do not follow a pattern page.

Does it work for knitting patterns?

Yes, the ruler and counters are not specific to crochet.

What if I tap twice by accident?

Double taps closer than a tenth of a second are ignored, and Undo reverts any step.
