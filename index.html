Rowby: Crochet Row Counter

Rowby is an iPhone app for counting rows while you crochet or knit: your pattern and your counter share one screen, and a ruler slides to the next row each time you tap. It works offline with no account, is free to start, and Rowby Pro adds the ruler, voice counting and unlimited projects.

Get Rowby on the App Store

Features

  • Pattern ruler. Add a PDF, photo or camera scan, set the ruler once, and it follows your count page after page (Pro).
  • Big count pad. Tap anywhere to add a row; undo any step; every tap is kept in a journal.
  • Hands-free counting. Say next, back or undo. Speech is recognised on the device (Pro).
  • Repeats and reminders. Repeat counters reset themselves; add counters for stitches, targets and row reminders.
  • Ten starter projects. Granny square, ripple blanket, beanie, market bag, socks, dishcloth and more.
  • Yarn stash. Weights, colours and what is left after each project (Pro).

Price

Free to start: your first project and first 30 counted rows. Rowby Pro (US prices): $29.99 per year with a 7-day free trial, $4.99 per month, or $59.99 once. No account, no cloud sync, no tracking; patterns stay on your iPhone.
